# Air Force Logistics - Design

Request supplies from the Quartermaster card. Free to request.

| Unit | Distance | Tier | Delivery | Load | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Peterson SFB, CO | 10 km | Truck | 1:00 | 12 | Level 1 |
| Hill AFB, UT | 800 km | Helicopter | 4:00 | 25 | Level 1 |
| Travis AFB, CA | 1,600 km | C-130 | 7:00 | 55 | Level 2 |
| Wright-Patterson AFB, OH | 1,900 km | C-130 | 8:00 | 70 | Level 3 |
| Ramstein AB, Germany | 8,000 km | C-17 | 14:00 | 125 | Level 4 |
| Kadena AB, Japan | 9,500 km | C-17 | 16:00 | 150 | Level 5 |

Tier cutoffs: Truck under 100 km, Helicopter 100 to 1,000 km, C-130 1,000 to 5,000 km, C-17 over 5,000 km.
Rules: one shipment per unit in transit; after any arrival all requests are blocked for that tier's cooldown (Truck 2:00, Helicopter 3:00, C-130 5:00, C-17 8:00); supplies auto-credit with a log entry.
Tel'tak base supply return raised from 40 to 100 in the same build.

Cap: no more than two shipments in transit at once (0.6.10).
