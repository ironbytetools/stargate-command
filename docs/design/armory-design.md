# Stargate Command - Armory Design

All rules below were decided by Brad. Ships in 0.5.9 as the Armory half of research phase two.

## Model
- Gear is researched in the Lab (research points, timed project, one project at a time), then bought with supplies, then issued to a team as a loadout in the Barracks.
- Each team has four slots: weapon, armor, pack, optics. Items are named, not tiered by purchase; each line is researched in order (like the Gate Room ladder).
- Weapons are per team type. Packs only matter to Exploration teams.

## Packs (carry cap on supplies per return)
Default Field Pack is free, cap 30. Biggest possible find is 90 (40 x 1.5 hazard x 1.5 two full trucks). Naquadah is outside the cap.

## Alien tech
- Minor items arrive as unidentified artifacts and are reverse-engineered in the Lab. One guaranteed artifact per level from a fixed world. Level 1 gives the Goa'uld healing device, Level 2 gives the staff weapon, later levels reserved.
- Healing device: speeds Infirmary healing.
- Tel'tak cargo ship: two ships, one from the Level 2 stronghold and one from the Level 3 stronghold, each restored in the Lab. Independent supply runs to a world the player selects. A run returns the world's maximum supply roll (40 x hazard), no carry cap. Travel is 2 minutes per light year, round trip total. Can be delayed, never ambushed or damaged.

## Deferred to a Sol Defense build
X-302 (Death Glider reverse-engineered), Ion Cannon, Tollan and Ancient defense tech. Story arc: Level 3 stronghold intel says the Goa'uld plan to attack Earth and raises DEFCON; the final Level 3 stronghold gives intel to track the Goa'uld fleet; a Sol Defense Status screen with a galactic fleet map, estimated time to attack and research state of defense projects; the arc ends when the last Level 4 stronghold is captured.

## Numbers settled during the build
- Weapons and optics use the same cost ladder as packs and armor by position in each line (100/200/300 RP, 2/4/6 minutes, 40/80/120 supplies).
- Alien items: staff weapon 300 RP, 6:00, 150 supplies; healing device 200 RP, 4:00, 150 supplies; each Tel'tak 600 RP, 12:00, 200 supplies.
- Gear is bought one unit per purchase and kept as stock until issued to a team.
- Infirmary base healing is 5:00. Maj. Hale halves it (2:30), and the healing device halves it again (1:15 with both).
- Tel'tak: run cost 2 supplies; round trip 2 minutes per light year; maximum supply roll (40 x hazard); delay 1 to 3 minutes on 1 run in 5; never ambushed or damaged.
- Artifacts: healing device from a fixed Level 1 world, staff weapon from a fixed Level 2 world; Tel'tak wrecks from a fixed stronghold in Level 2 and in Level 3. Levels 3 to 5 artifacts are reserved.
