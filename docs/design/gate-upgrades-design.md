# Stargate Command - Gate Room Upgrade Path

Decided by Brad. Ships in 0.5.9 with research phase two.

## Rules
- One shared tier ladder (Gate Room tier 0 to 7). Each tier needs its research project finished in the Lab before it can be advanced to.
- Security and Harriman and the dialing speed tiers: research unlocks the tier, then the supply purchase is instant. Reactor tiers are the research itself (research points, no supplies).
- The ladder lives in the Gate Room card in the Facility; research projects start from the Lab project list like MALP tiers.
- Research uses the Lab's one-project engine (scientist bonus, rush at 20 RP per minute).

## Ladder
| Tier | Upgrade | Research | Time | Purchase | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gate security detail | 100 RP | 2:00 | 150 supplies | Security personnel in the gate room stop intruders from stealing supplies |
| 2 | Chief Master Sergeant Walter Harriman at the iris | 200 RP | 4:00 | 200 supplies | Fully operates the iris: opens it when a valid IDC transponder is received, closes it on a hostile signal |
| 3 | Dialing speed I | 300 RP | 5:00 | 100 supplies | Auto-dial step 90 to 60 ms, lock pause 550 to 400 ms |
| 4 | Reactor I | 1000 RP | 6:00 | none | Max power 100 to 200 |
| 5 | Dialing speed II | 500 RP | 8:00 | 200 supplies | Auto-dial step 45 ms, lock pause 250 ms |
| 6 | Reactor II | 1000 RP | 10:00 | none | Max power 300 |
| 7 | Reactor III | 1000 RP | 14:00 | none | Max power 400 |
