# Easter eggs and fun minigames (design notes, Oct 26 2026)

## Picked: staff chatter (drafts for approval)
Rare idle Ops log lines, original wording, from permanent staff only while they are hired and on station. Spacing: one line every 8 to 15 minutes of play, never during a timed minigame.
- Hammond: "Remind the teams that borrowed equipment still needs a requisition." / "Someone is leaving coffee rings on the briefing table again." / "Headquarters wants a report on last week's report." / "I would like one quiet mission before the end of the quarter."
- Carter: "Power readings are within tolerance. Mostly." / "I have three ideas for the reactor, and only one of them is loud." / "The gate is fine. It is whatever is on the other side that worries me." / "Has anyone seen my good soldering iron?"
- Lee: "For the record, I did not touch the calibration. It was like that when I arrived." / "The coffee machine on Level 19 is making that noise again." / "If the lab smells like burnt toast, that is a feature."
- Jackson (Archeology Office, once hired): "I found a typo in a three-thousand-year-old inscription. Scribes were people too." / "Does anyone know where the good dictionaries went?" / "This glyph looks familiar. That is either very good news or very bad news."

## Picked: gate address nods (proposal)
The dialing computer selects destinations from the starmap, so free-form address entry is not possible. Nods hang on existing events instead.
- Glyph hover names: each symbol in the sequence compiler names the constellation it stands for (true to the show, where symbols are constellations).
- First dial to each system's gate prints a short address readout line in the Ops log.
- Redialing the same world many times in a row draws a dry Carter remark about wear on the ring.
- A Level-secured system's first dial afterward adds a one-line "address now in the permanent record" note.
Open: whether these should be on by default or switchable in Settings.

## Parked: Hide the SGC (Briefing Room visitor minigame)
Decided: cover-story mechanic (visitors ask pointed questions, the player picks consistent cover stories from a short list, played on a clipboard and visitor-log device in the Briefing Room panel); rare random event the player may decline; failure halves supplies earned from the AF Logistics bases for 10 minutes; comic Ops log fallout on every outcome.
Open: what makes the player accept a visit. With no success bonus, declining is free, so options are decline carries the same cut, a small thank-you bonus on a pass, or neither. Brad shelved it Oct 26 to revisit later.
