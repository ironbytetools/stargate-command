# Stargate Command - Group Missions, Security Teams and Peaceful Worlds

All rules below were decided by Brad. Built in 0.5.9.

## Mission types
The player picks a mission type in the gate room before adding teams. The team dropdown only offers teams the type accepts.
- Exploration: Exploration, Recon and Security teams on a regular world. Needs an Exploration team. Cannot go to a known stronghold.
- Recon Survey: Recon teams only, to any world.
- Assault: Assault, Security and Recon teams against a known stronghold, plus an optional Exploration team. Needs an Assault or Security team.
- Assist: Recon, Assault or Security teams sent to rescue an overdue team (existing assist rules). Sent with SEND ASSIST.
- MALP stays a separate launch that can ride along or go alone.

## Group rules
- Every team sent together is one mission with one mission log entry and one return.
- One ambush roll for the whole group. The group's chance is the lowest chance among its teams (best protection), from world hazard, Recon count, vehicles, optics and camaraderie.
- A delay applies to everyone. Each team rolls its own injury (its gear and camaraderie). A group goes overdue as one, and contact, recall and assist act on the whole group.
- Teams do not go overdue on worlds below Level 2. Ambushes there still delay and injure.

## Security team
- Air Force Security Forces, 4 roles like Assault (Team Lead, Rifleman, Machine Gunner, Grenadier). Raise 25 supplies, send 5. Limited to the current level like other teams.
- On an Exploration mission, any Security team in the group neutralizes 80% of ambushes (no delay, no injury, no overdue). Flat 80%, no stacking.
- In assaults a Security team counts as 95% of a team in the team-size score and its weapon points are 95%. It earns camaraderie and takes casualty rolls like Assault. On Exploration and Diplomacy missions it earns 10 camaraderie, or 5 if injured, same as Recon.
- Uses the Assault weapon line, armor, optics and the Jaffa staff weapon.

## Alien technology and assaults
- An Exploration team in an assault group is optional. It is required to recover alien technology from the captured stronghold.
- If no Exploration team was present, the wreck is not recovered at capture. The stronghold is flagged for salvage, and an Exploration mission to that captured stronghold recovers it.

## Peaceful worlds
- A captured stronghold becomes Peaceful. Peaceful worlds have no ambushes. Intel and the starmap show a PEACEFUL badge and ambush risk NONE.
- Planned: other worlds can become Peaceful through diplomatic missions (see the roadmap). The game already treats a world flagged peaceful the same way.
