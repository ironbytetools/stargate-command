# Special Operations Rescue Teams - Design

Status: built in 0.6.14. Numbers approved by Brad.

## Agreed

- Requested from the Quartermaster card, same style of timing and usage as the Air Force Logistics requests.
- Five Special Operations teams, real US units, one unlocking per level, strongest last.
- A request names its target when called. Targets: an overdue team, or a captured member held on a stronghold.
- The request is free. A supply fee is paid when the team is sent through the gate.
- The team travels from its base to the SGC. On arrival the gate dials out on its own, visibly, and the team goes through.
- A rescue always succeeds. It can be delayed; the higher the team, the lower the delay chance; the top team is never delayed.
- After a rescue the team returns and can be requested again at once.
- Rescue requests have their own limits, separate from logistics.
- Freeing captured members also closes the Level 1 dead end where the Assault and Security teams both hold a captured member.

Travel time to the SGC is set by level, not distance: 5:00 at Level 1 stepping down 1:00 per level to 1:00 at Level 5. Base locations are flavor only. Delay length 1 to 3 minutes, as ambush and Tel'tak delays. Rescue mission length 2 minutes, as an assist. One request per unit at a time.

## Assumptions to confirm

- The dial-out costs normal gate power for the target (cost plus the 5 auto-dial); if the gate is busy or power is short, the team waits and dials when it can.
- An overdue team's 15-minute self-return timer pauses while a rescue team is en route, as it does for assist teams.
- A rescued team has no yield cut and heals on arrival, as in assist missions; a freed captured member returns ready.
- One rescue team can recover everything on its target world (an overdue team and any captured members there).
