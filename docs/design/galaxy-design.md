# Stargate Command - Galaxy and Starmap Design

Status: built in 0.5.8. Decisions below were made by Brad.

## Structure
- Each level is one real star system in the Milky Way. System names use real stars throughout; planets are fictional.
- Our solar system (Sol) is on the map as home.
- The starmap gets two layers: a galaxy view (Milky Way chart with Sol marked, systems lit as they are reached) and a system view (the worlds of the current level, as the starmap shows today). Old levels stay dialable through the all-levels view.
- World counts per system are authored in a data table, not generated by a formula, so they vary per system. Every world keeps its own six-symbol address plus origin.
- The starmap needs a visual overhaul to support both layers.
- System view is an orbital diagram with moons and orbital stations as destinations as well as planets.
- Build slot: 0.5.8. Research phase two moves to 0.5.9.
- Scale note: nearby real stars sit within about 12 light-years of Sol, which is a speck at galaxy scale, so the galaxy view shows a schematic local-neighborhood zoom around Sol.

## Galaxy chart look
- Flat star chart (mockup B in ../../mockups/galaxy-style-b.png): galactic-coordinate rings and longitude ticks, spiral arm outlines, Sol marked, and a sector list of the five systems with level, distance and status.

## Systems (approved)
Level 1 Alpha Centauri (4.4 ly), Level 2 Barnard's Star (6.0 ly), Level 3 Sirius (8.6 ly), Level 4 Epsilon Eridani (10.5 ly), Level 5 Tau Ceti (11.9 ly).

## Travel paths
- Live path in both layers: a marker moves along a line on the galaxy view for the leg between systems, and on the system view for the leg to the planet. It travels back the same way when the team returns through the gate. An overdue team's line stays lit.

## Distance and cost
- Distance affects gate power cost, set per system (a fixed authored cost, no formula). Level 1 costs today's base gate cost; each level increases the cost by 10% (this replaced an earlier 5% answer). Level 5 is the final level, so the game has five systems.
- Open: whether the 10% steps are linear or compounding, whether auto-dial's extra cost scales too.
- What happens when Level 5 is completed is decided later.

## Open
- World counts per system, and the world naming scheme
- Moons and stations: how many per world, and whether the gate sits on a planet or a station

## Decisions (latest round)
- Gate power cost is fixed per system and linear: Level 1 = 10, Level 2 = 20, Level 3 = 30, Level 4 = 40, Level 5 = 50 (replaces the earlier +10% idea).
- Auto-dial extra cost changes to a flat 5 on every level.
- World naming: invented names with the real star as the sector (for example Kalos in Tau Ceti); moons and stations are named off their parent world.
- Open: world count and stronghold count for each of the five systems (Brad to supply).

## World counts (Brad)
- L1 Alpha Centauri: 5 planets, 1 stronghold, 1 faction
- L2 Barnard's Star: 7 planets, 2 strongholds, 1 faction planet
- L3 Sirius: 5 planets, 3 strongholds
- L4 Epsilon Eridani: 6 planets, 1 stronghold, 2 faction
