# Gate Power - Design

Cost = 8 power per light year of the world's distance, rounded. Distance = the star system distance plus an orbit-order offset from -0.4 ly (innermost world) to +0.4 ly (outermost), spread evenly.

| Level | System | Distance | Worlds | Cost range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Alpha Centauri | 4.4 ly | 5 | 32 to 38 |
| 2 | Barnard's Star | 6.0 ly | 7 | 45 to 51 |
| 3 | Sirius | 8.6 ly | 5 | 66 to 72 |
| 4 | Epsilon Eridani | 10.5 ly | 6 | 81 to 87 |
| 5 | Tau Ceti | 11.9 ly | 10 | 92 to 98 |

Power: capped at 120 (0.6.12). Starts at 80. Regeneration 12 per minute, Reactor I 24, Reactor II 36, Reactor III 48. Failed lock costs 2, auto-dial adds 5, naquadah converts to +15.

Ancient Research Ship: revealed when the Level 5 Ancient Lab is explored. Special one-off peaceful world, Tau Ceti system, gate cost 98. An Exploration team recovers the ZPM there. The ZPM is installed onto the reactor in the Quartermaster. Pegasus travel needs it installed; effects and Atlantis cost to be decided.
