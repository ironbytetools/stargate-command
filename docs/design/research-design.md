# Stargate Command - Research Design

All rules below were decided by Brad.

## Engine
- Research is a timed project run from the Science Lab. One project at a time. More slots may come later as a Lab upgrade; no waiting queue.
- Vehicles stay instant purchases in the Motor Pool and are unchanged.
- A project costs research points and supplies, paid up front when it starts.
- Base pace: the project time in the table. A flat 15% reduction in time applies while at least one scientist or linguist is on station in the Lab. Scientists away or injured means base pace. Research never pauses.
- Rush: pay 20 research points per minute of remaining time (rounded up) to finish a project immediately. The remaining time counts the 15% reduction when it applies.
- No Lab upgrade track in this phase.

## MALP tiers (per unit)
Each MALP carries its own tier. A rebuilt MALP starts at tier 0. A MALP being upgraded sits in the Lab and cannot launch until the project finishes. Mission speed is not affected (that stays exclusive to the ATV). Extra MALP capacity is dropped for now.

| Tier | Research points | Supplies | Project time | Return odds when ambushed | Intel revealed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base (0) | none | none | none | 30% | 1 |
| 1 | 100 | 50 | 2:00 | 20% | 1 |
| 2 | 200 | 100 | 6:30 | 40% | 2 |
| 3 | 300 | 150 | 11:00 | 60% | 3 |
| 4 | 400 | 200 | 15:30 | 80% | 4 |
| 5 | 500 | 250 | 20:00 | 100% | 5 |

## Intel
A fifth intel tier was added: stronghold defenses. Intel 1 to 4 are unchanged (type and environment, stronghold flag, ambush risk, difficulty band). Recon surveys still reveal 1 to 4. Only a tier 5 MALP reveals tier 5.

## Sending a MALP
The player chooses which MALP at home launches, from buttons in the gate room send panel showing each unit and its tier.

## Later phases (all chosen as unlock types)
- Armory equipment
- Base upgrades (reactor, shielding, extra rooms, Lab upgrades and extra project slots)
- Alien technology: minor items from regular exploration, major items from stronghold captures
